About me

I’m a web developer in Lahore who builds practical digital solutions for businesses: websites, online stores and the internal tools that keep a business running.

I start with the problem, not the technology. For BizFlow that meant asking what a shop owner needs to know each day (stock, unpaid invoices, expenses, profit) and building the data model and screens around those answers.

I enjoy learning modern technologies and working closely with clients to understand their requirements. I use AI tools in my workflow, and I review, test and debug everything before it ships.
